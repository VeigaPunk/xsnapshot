xsnapshot

Your X profile, persistent and crawlable. X walls your posts off from the web — but the archive is yours. Export it, submit it, and it becomes a normal website: indexable by any crawler, searchable, permanent.

1Request your official X archive download (Settings → Your account → Download an archive).
2Run the generator: node cli.js /path/to/archive ./out
3Your snapshot lives at xsnapshot.app/@handle/ — sitemap, JSON-LD, full-text data, robots-welcome.
